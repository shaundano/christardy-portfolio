Directed study under Dr. Frank Wood, UBC. Project Elephant wraps the NICE DCV Web Client SDK for remote classroom and lab sessions. Teachers and students get role based access to a full desktop session in the browser, with automated authentication and fullscreen launch on connect.

OCAP streams keyboard, mouse, and video from each session to S3. That feeds a scalable pipeline for capturing AI training data from any desktop app running on a cloud machine.
