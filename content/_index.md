I am a PhD-track MSc student at the [University of British Columbia](https://www.ubc.ca/) (UBC). My background spans networks, backend engineering, and applied AI, most recently as a GenAI software engineering intern at Planview. I work on **world action models (WAMs)**, focusing on positional embeddings (RoPE variations) and latent diffusion optimizations, aiming to improve robustness of action-conditioned video frame tracking.

I previously earned a Bachelor of Commerce from Concordia University, then spent two years in marketing at [Sid Lee](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=iQrTm6ShxN0&list=RDiQrTm6ShxN0&start_radio=1).
