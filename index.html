About

I'm a PhD-track MSc student at UBC. My background spans networks, backend engineering, and applied AI — most recently as a GenAI software engineering intern at Planview.

I contributed to PLAICraft, a project collecting multimodal AI training data from Minecraft, and received an acknowledgement in the accompanying paper. My undergraduate capstone, Project Elephant, generalized that idea: any desktop app accessible via a cloud machine can feed a scalable framework for AI training data capture.

Research

I currently work on world action models (WAMs), focusing on positional embeddings (RoPE variations) and latent diffusion optimizations. My immediate goal is improving robustness of action-conditioned video frame tracking, including ballistic mouse movement.
